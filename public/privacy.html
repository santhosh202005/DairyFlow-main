DAIRY FLOW – PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: 5 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Dairy Flow, a dairy management application developed by Santrix Tech.

Dairy Flow helps dairy vendors, farmers and customers manage milk supply, daily transactions, billing, payments, cattle feed purchases and related activities.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, shared and protected when you use Dairy Flow.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your use of the application, we may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Address
  • Account and login information

Milk Supply Information

  • Daily morning (AM) and evening (PM) milk entries
  • Milk quantities and rates
  • Customer and vendor details
  • Milk supply history

Financial Information

  • Monthly bills
  • Payment and transaction records
  • Advance payments
  • Outstanding balances
  • Cattle feed purchases and deductions

Technical Information

  • Device and application information
  • Application usage information
  • Error and diagnostic information, where collected

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Verify users through OTP authentication.
  • Record daily milk supply.
  • Calculate monthly bills.
  • Manage customer and vendor transactions.
  • Generate PDF bills, receipts and statements.
  • Send important service notifications.
  • Improve application performance and security.
  • Provide customer support.

4. OTP and SMS Notifications

Dairy Flow may use third-party SMS service providers, including Fast2SMS, for OTP verification and authorized service communications.

Your mobile number may be used for:

  • Account verification.
  • Login OTP delivery.
  • Relevant service announcements, where permitted.

Dairy Flow may also provide notifications through its in-app notification system.

5. Data Storage and Security

Dairy Flow uses backend infrastructure, database services and hosting providers to store and process application information.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure and loss.

Access to information is controlled according to application roles and permissions.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Information Sharing

Santrix Tech does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate Dairy Flow, including:

  • Database and hosting providers.
  • SMS and OTP service providers.
  • Other technical service providers required for application functionality.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or lawful government requests.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide Dairy Flow services, maintain transaction history, meet applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Some financial and transaction records may need to be retained even after an account deletion request, where required by applicable law.

Information that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible.

8. Account Deletion and User Rights

Dairy Flow users can request deletion of their accounts through the application.

Users may also contact our support team to request:

  • Access to their personal information.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Withdrawal of consent where applicable.
  • Assistance with privacy-related concerns.

Account deletion process:

  1. Open Dairy Flow.
  2. Navigate to Settings or Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the deletion information.
  5. Confirm your deletion request.
  6. Complete any required identity verification.

After verification, the deletion request will be processed within the applicable legal timeframe.

Account deletion may result in the loss of access to account information and services. Certain financial or transaction records may be retained where legally required.

9. Children's Privacy

Dairy Flow is intended for individuals using the application for dairy-related business and services. It is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information without the required authorization.

10. Third-Party Services

Dairy Flow may use third-party services for hosting, database storage, OTP verification and SMS delivery.

These service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, services or legal requirements change.

Updated versions will be published within the application and on our public website, with a revised Last Updated date.
